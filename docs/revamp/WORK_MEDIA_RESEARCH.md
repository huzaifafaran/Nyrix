## Safe visual direction for implementation

1. **Best option, if Nyrix has source access:** capture authentic, sanitized screens from the actual Genix EMS and TeachMeBack product at a useful resolution. Remove private facility data, student material, usernames and identifiers. Keep an internal provenance note identifying who supplied each capture and the capture date. These can be called product screens.
2. **If genuine screens are unavailable now:** commission distinct conceptual campaign images and label them as illustrative. For Genix, use a restrained macro photograph or original rendering of a cleanroom environmental sensor, particle counter, controlled corridor or instrument detail, with no invented dashboard readings, Genix branding or compliance badge. For TeachMeBack, use a quiet editorial composition of study notes and an abstract exchange of questions, or a real crop of its public landing page explicitly captioned as the public website. Avoid generated mock product UI, fabricated device screens, and a generic purple ribbon reused from the hero.
3. **Card treatment from the supplied reference:** large media rectangle above a white editorial text panel, broad gap between cards, understated category line, title, concise project-specific summary and clear case-study link. Use the same case-specific visual on its detail page with a different crop or scale; that is continuity, not unrelated asset reuse. The graphic language can share Nyrix white/black typography and restrained violet material accents without duplicating the hero artwork.
